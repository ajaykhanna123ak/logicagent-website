How we work

A short path from idea to production

No open-ended discovery. Each phase ends in something you can review, run, or hand to your team.

  1. 01

    Scope

    A working session on the problem, data, and constraints. You leave with an architecture and a fixed-scope proposal.

  2. 02

    Prototype

    A narrow end-to-end slice on your real data, with an evaluation set — so the decision to continue is based on numbers.

  3. 03

    Build

    Production hardening: infrastructure, CI/CD, guardrails, cost controls, and the tests that keep quality from drifting.

  4. 04

    Hand over

    Documentation, dashboards, and a walkthrough with your engineers. Ongoing support is optional, never a dependency.
